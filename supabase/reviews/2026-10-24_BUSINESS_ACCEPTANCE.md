
required. It refuses production/wrong project/run/accounts before writes and

the dedicated private fixture credentials; never rotates a password or adopts an

Current transition_booking enforces actor/status/cancellation/evidence, not the
scheduled wall-clock time. Thus this future synthetic appointment could be started
and completed on the acceptance day. No time was backdated/overridden; no Staging-
only bypass was added. Reports correctly use actual completion history timestamp,
not the future appointment date, for their ranges.
